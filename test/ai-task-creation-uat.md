## Scope notes

- Source-image cropping, external link cards and language-investigation selection require teacher-supplied sources or setup; task creation does not invent them.
- A PDF, a game, a storyboard, a saved question bank item and teacher-owned video remain dedicated workflows rather than AI-generated inline content. Recordings and pictures use the task media preparation path.
- Teacher-training form kinds apply only to teacher-training classes; test them separately with a training profile.
- A green result requires the generated draft, private key, saved teacher editor, learner view and interaction to work on the published build. Local schema/adapter tests alone are not a green result.
