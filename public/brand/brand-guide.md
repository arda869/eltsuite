# ELT Suite

Tools for English language teachers. Calm, practical and on the teacher's side: a good colleague in the staffroom, not a tech product.

## The look in one line

Lots of white, warm greys, one terracotta accent, a serif for what people read and a sans for what they press.

## Logo

The mark is a rounded terracotta square with a white "E" built from four rounded bars. The logo is the mark plus "ELT Suite" in Karla ExtraBold.

- **Logo** (mark + name): the default, on white or light grounds.
- **Logo on dark**: the same with a light name, on `bg` dark or photos with a dark area.
- **Logo on accent**: white mark and white name, on a solid `accent` ground.
- **Stacked logo**: mark above the name, for square spaces (posters, slides' title pages).
- **Mark**: on its own where space is small or the name is already nearby (app icon, favicon, email signature, avatars).
- **Mono marks**: one colour (ink, white or accent) for stamps, embossing, watermarks and one-colour print.

Rules:
- Leave clear space around the logo equal to half the mark's height.
- Smallest sizes: the mark 16px on screen, the logo 24px tall on screen or 8mm in print.
- Don't recolour the mark, stretch it, add shadows, outline it or put it on a busy picture.
- An organisation's own logo replaces ours on its worksheets and emails; ours then stays small in the footer or not at all.

## Colour

- **Accent `accent` (#d98f63)** is the brand. Use it sparingly: one button, one rule, numbers. Never as a large background behind reading text.
- **Accent text `accent-text`** for links and small coloured text, because plain accent is too light to read at small sizes.
- **Ink `ink`** for text, **muted `muted`** for secondary text. Never pure black.
- **Warm greys** (`surface`, `track`, `hairline`) for panels and lines. Borders, not shadows.
- **Right `right` and wrong `wrong`** only ever mean right and wrong answers.
- Every screen works in light and dark. Print is always light (`print-band`, `print-ink`).

## Type

- **Fraunces** (serif) for everything people read: headings, lesson text, worksheet text, email body in apps that show it.
- **Karla** (sans) for everything people press or scan: buttons, labels, menus, kickers, numbers in chips, footers.
- **Kickers**: Karla 800, 12px, uppercase, letter-spaced, in `accent-text`.
- **Emails**: brand fonts load in Apple Mail and some others; Gmail and Outlook fall back to Georgia (reading) and Arial (labels). Personal emails use Gmail's default sans and default black.
- Two typefaces only. The clearer-font option swaps both for Atkinson Hyperlegible.

## Writing

- "You" for the reader. "Teachers" and "learners", never "students" or "users". "We" only for the ELT Suite team.
- Short sentences, everyday words, British English (organisation, colour, enrol, practise).
- Sentence case everywhere. Buttons are verbs that say what happens: "Make a class", "Send invitation".
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype ("amazing", "seamless", "powerful").
- Errors say what happened, what to do, and that work is safe.

## Shapes and spacing

- Corners: 10px buttons and fields (`radius-md`), 18px cards (`radius-lg`), pills for badges and answer chips (`radius-pill`).
- The mark's corners are a quarter of its size (`radius-mark`).
- Spacing steps 4, 8, 16, 28, 40px. Generous white space over dense layouts.

## Where it's used

- **The app**: white or warm-charcoal ground, terracotta for the one main action.
- **Emails**: logo above a white card with a thin terracotta line on top, terracotta buttons, a short grey footer.
- **Worksheets**: like a coursebook photocopiable. Grey header band, class colour as the one accent, Fraunces for learners' text, Karla for labels.
- **Social**: the square avatar (terracotta with the white mark) and the share image (logo and tagline on warm grey).
- **Email signature**: the mark, name, role, tagline and eltsuite.com in Arial.
