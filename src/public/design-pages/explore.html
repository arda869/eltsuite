Plan

Build a lesson that holds together

Organise classes into units, lessons and tasks. Combine reading, listening, speaking and written work. Add questions, language examples and notes for yourself, then preview what learners will see.

The small teaching details are built in: word and sentence stress, phonemic symbols, timelines, substitution tables and reveals. Use them to make the language you are teaching easier to notice.

Prepare work for the whole class or adapt tasks for different learners and groups. Print supported activities and teaching notes when paper suits the lesson.

Teach

Keep the activity at the centre

Open your prepared lesson and teach live. Guide learners through the activities, see their responses and choose when to reveal answers. Use discussion, shared work and classroom tools when they support the task.

Learners join your class using the details you share with them. You can teach alongside the calling tools you already use.

For work outside class, set homework, repeatable practice or an exam. Add a time limit where useful and choose when to release exam results.

Understand

Look beyond a score

Review a response, add a mark or comment and keep useful work in a learner's portfolio. Class and learner reports bring recorded results together so you can spot something worth revisiting.

Where AI learning reports are enabled, suggestions can help you reflect on the available work. Treat them as a starting point for your judgement, alongside what you know from the classroom.

Make materials

Give the lesson what it needs

Make the pictures, recordings and games your lesson calls for, then bring them into Classes.

DesignerMake pictures, labelled scenes and storyboards to use in Classes. Arrange characters, objects and speech bubbles on the canvas. Reuse saved visuals and, where enabled, generate or adapt an image with AI.
RecordingsRecord and edit your own audio, work with a script and bring the result into a listening task. Record a dialogue with colleagues, each voice on its own track.
VideoEdit classroom clips and prepare material around them. AI video generation, where enabled, is a separate option with its own limits.
Quiz showPrepare a reusable set of questions and run a quiz activity with your class.
Material importBring in supported files, review the extracted content and turn suitable material into editable lesson activities. Check the result before using it.
Collaborate

Build on one another's work

Work on documents together in Collab. Add comments, suggest changes and keep the teaching notation you use in class inside the document. Shared slides are also available to explore in beta.

Use shared PDFs for annotation. Keep reusable lessons, documents, recordings and other resources organised in folders. Share the right material with colleagues and continue the discussion in Messages.

Stay organised

Pick up where you left off

Use Timetable to see classes and meetings across the week. Keep your teaching resources together and return to recent work from Home. Your conversations, class materials and shared files remain close to the work they support.

Start with the part you need

You do not have to use every tool at once. Begin with a lesson, a resource or a question about your teaching, then build from there.
