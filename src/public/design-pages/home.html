Built around the work you already do

A lesson is more than the material on the screen. It is the thinking before it, the responses during it and the decisions you make afterwards. ELT Suite brings these parts of teaching closer together.

Plan·Teach·Understand·Make·Share·Grow
01 · Plan

Plan with a clear purpose

Build your lesson around its aims. Bring text, questions, pictures and recordings together, with your teaching notes beside each activity. Start with your own ideas, adapt existing materials or use AI for suggestions.

Explore lesson planning →
02 · Teach

Teach from the lesson you prepared

Present an activity, reveal the next step and follow learners' answers as they arrive. Use the lesson in class, teach online or set work for learners to complete afterwards.

Explore classroom tools →
03 · Understand

Let learner work inform what comes next

Look at submitted answers, leave feedback and review results across lessons. Return to an individual learner's work when you need a closer look, then decide what to revisit with the class.

Explore feedback and progress →
04 · Make

Make materials that fit your lesson

Build a visual scene in Designer. Record a dialogue, shape a listening activity or prepare a quiz show. Bring the elements you need into Classes and keep them ready to use again.

Explore creative tools →
05 · Share

Good ideas are worth sharing

Write a lesson plan with a colleague. Improve a shared resource. Keep the conversation going in Messages. Collab gives you a place to work on the document together, with language teaching tools close at hand.

Explore collaboration →
06 · Grow

Keep your own learning close

Keep reading notes and references, explore a question about language or reflect on a lesson. Use Teacher's Companion, where enabled, to think through a question and consider what you might try next.

Explore professional learning →
