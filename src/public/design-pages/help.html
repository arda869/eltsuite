Getting access

How do teachers join?

Teacher accounts currently need an invitation. Open the invitation link you received, or choose "I have an invitation" on the sign-in page and follow the instructions.

I already have an account. Where do I go?

Choose "Sign in" on the teacher website. Use the sign-in method and account you normally use.

How do learners join a class?

Use the class link or code your teacher gives you. Choose your name and enter the PIN your teacher provided, or the password you have already set. The details can vary by class.

I am a learner and cannot get in.

Ask your teacher to check your class details. Learner access is separate from teacher invitations.

Using the tools

Do I need to use every app?

No. Start with one thing you want to do, such as making a lesson, organising a resource or keeping reading notes. The Guide inside your account can help you choose a starting point.

Can I use ELT Suite on a phone?

Many everyday tasks, including learner activities, messages and live teaching controls, are designed for smaller screens. Detailed editing tools work best on a computer or tablet. Some editors will tell you when they need a larger screen.

Can I teach in person?

Yes. Present a lesson in class, use learner activities where appropriate or print supported material.

Can I bring my existing materials?

You can upload supported resources and use material-import tools where available. Review imported text, questions and formatting before teaching, and make sure you have permission to use the source.

Do I have to use AI?

No. You can write, edit and teach with your own materials. Optional AI tools are available in particular workflows and may depend on your account settings and allowances.

What can Teacher's Companion see?

It can use supported teaching context according to its settings, including relevant lesson information and visible page context. It does not automatically read every learner record or every standalone Reading Journal entry. See AI and your teaching for more detail.

Is Communities available?

Communities is planned. Current collaboration happens through connected colleagues, shared resources, Collab and Messages.

Something is not working

Tell us what you were trying to do, which page you were on and what happened. Include your browser and device if you can. A screenshot can help, provided it does not contain private learner information.
