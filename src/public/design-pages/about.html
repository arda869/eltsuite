About ELT Suite

Created out of a love for English language teaching

ELT Suite grew from a simple belief: teachers deserve tools that understand their work, and opportunities to learn from one another.

We are bringing planning, classroom practice, materials, assessment and professional learning into one connected space.

Teachers bring the judgement

Every class is different. Teachers bring knowledge, experience, creativity and an understanding of their learners. Good tools should make that expertise easier to use.

That is why ELT Suite keeps the teacher involved in the choices that matter, from shaping an activity to deciding what to revisit after a lesson.

We learn from one another

A useful idea often starts with a conversation. A colleague shares a resource. Someone asks a question. Another teacher offers a different way of looking at it.

We want those exchanges to have a place alongside the practical work of teaching.

Built with care, and still growing

We are starting with invited teachers and developing the platform as we learn. Some tools are available now; others are still taking shape. We will be clear about the difference.

Our aim is to keep useful teaching and collaboration accessible while building a sustainable service for the long term.

Our mission

Bring the tools, knowledge and collaborative opportunities English language teachers need into one connected space.
