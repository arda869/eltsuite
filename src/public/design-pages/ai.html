AI and your teaching

Useful support. Your judgement.

ELT Suite includes AI tools for specific teaching tasks, where they are enabled. They can help you draft material, explore an idea or reflect on your work. You decide how the result fits your learners and your purpose.

Where AI can help

Depending on your access, AI tools can support these tasks. Each tool has a particular job; availability and usage limits can differ.

What information a feature uses

The information used depends on the task. It may include your prompt, material you upload or select, and relevant teaching context that the feature is permitted to access.

Teacher's Companion uses the teaching context available to your account and conversation, including relevant lessons and documents, according to your settings. It does not have unrestricted access to private learner records.

Some other features process learner responses for a specific purpose, such as speaking feedback or a learning report. This is separate from the Companion's context settings.

Check the result before using it

AI can miss context, produce an inaccurate explanation or suggest material that does not suit your class. Check language, facts, level, instructions, answer keys and suitability before you use or share a result.

An AI suggestion about learner work is one source of information. Use it alongside the actual response and what you know about the learner.

Services used to provide AI

Some features send the information needed for the task to external service providers. The Privacy page will explain the services in use, the relevant information and the applicable data arrangements.
