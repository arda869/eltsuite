Use what you need. See what it costs.

Our planned credit system will show a clear price for each supported AI action before you choose to use it. Different actions will use different amounts, depending on what they do.

Teachers will receive credits through eligible subscriptions and will be able to buy extra credits separately. Under the planned policy, unused monthly credits will expire at renewal and purchased credits will not expire. Learner AI activities will use the responsible teacher's balance.

This credit system is not available to buy or use as a paid wallet yet. Current trial allowances may work differently.

Keeping the essentials accessible

Our aim is to keep essential teaching and collaboration tools accessible. Some services, including AI generation, media processing and storage, have ongoing costs. Paid plans and credits will help fund those services and the continued development of ELT Suite.

A few practical questions

Can I buy a plan now?

Not yet. Teachers currently join by invitation. We will publish prices and purchasing terms before subscriptions open.

Is there a free plan?

A Free plan is proposed. Its final features and allowances are still being confirmed.

How much will an AI action cost?

The credit prices are still being set. The planned system will show the price before you confirm an action.

Will monthly and purchased credits work differently?

Yes. The planned policy is that monthly credits expire at renewal and purchased credits do not expire. This policy will apply when the paid credit system launches.

Will learners have to buy credits?

The planned model uses their teacher's balance for learner AI activities. Learner spending controls and the exact allocation rules will be explained before launch.

What about video calls, storage and learner limits?

These will be listed separately in the final plans. A credit allowance should not be read as unlimited access to every service.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my work?

Paid subscriptions are not open yet. Cancellation, refunds and access to stored work after a plan ends will be explained before anyone subscribes.
