Questions

A few practical answers.

The prices and allowances on this page are proposed while ELT Suite is invitation-only.

Can I use ELT Suite for free?

Yes. Free includes three active classes, 1 GB of storage, our planning and classroom tools, assessment and collaboration. You also get 100 monthly guidance replies and 1,000 welcome AI credits to try creation tools. No card is required and there is no automatic paid upgrade.

What is the difference between Teacher and Teacher Plus?

Both have the same teaching tools, 30 active classes, 10 GB of storage, included guidance and 180 automatic transcription minutes. Teacher Plus adds 1,000 AI creation credits every month. Teacher costs US$15 per month; Teacher Plus costs US$20.

Do I need to pay for teacher development or product help?

No. Every plan includes 100 replies each month across Teacher’s Companion and product help, with no credits deducted. This is for reflection, feedback on your teaching and help using ELT Suite. Asking a creation tool to generate materials uses the separate credit prices shown above. Your remaining guidance allowance is visible.

Can I use AI without a subscription?

Yes. Use your welcome credits or purchase a top-up on Free. The same creation tariffs apply to every plan. Your plan’s class and storage limits still apply.

Is the welcome offer worth US$20?

It gives you the same 1,000 AI credits as a month of the US$20 Teacher Plus plan. It does not include a paid-plan subscription or US$20 of cash credit. At our top-up rate, 1,000 credits cost US$10. The welcome balance expires after 30 days.

What happens when credits run out, and do they expire?

AI creation pauses until you have enough credits; your saved work remains. Plus monthly credits expire at the next renewal. Welcome and loyalty credits expire after 30 days. Purchased credits expire 12 months after each purchase. The wallet shows all expiry dates and uses the earliest-expiring balance first.

Can my learners spend my credits?

Only on eligible activity you enable. Their use is charged to your balance and stays within the spending limit you set. You can review the activity and switch off further spending.

How do cancellation and plan changes work?

Cancel before the next renewal in your account to stop that payment. You keep the paid plan until the end of the current period, then move to Free. Cancellation partway through a month does not refund the unused days. Requested upgrades and downgrades take effect at the next renewal, with the new price and allowance shown before confirmation. Billing mistakes and refund requests can be sent to arda@eltsuite.com.

Will I lose my work if I downgrade?

No. Moving to a smaller plan does not delete your saved lessons, files or learner records. When you exceed the new plan’s class or storage limit, you can view and export your work, but must archive classes or free space before adding more. Purchased credits keep their original expiry dates.

Do you offer annual billing or a loyalty reward?

We bill monthly only. After 12 consecutive paid months, the next month is free and you receive 1,000 bonus AI credits, valid for 30 days. Teacher Plus also receives its usual monthly credits in the free month. Changing between paid plans keeps your progress; moving to Free or a payment lapse resets it. The reward repeats after another 12 paid months. Your account shows your progress and next charge date.

What currency will I be charged in?

US dollars. Any applicable tax and the final total appear before payment. Your bank may charge for currency conversion.
